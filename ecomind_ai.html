About EcoMind AI

1M1B AI for Sustainability Virtual Internship · IBM SkillsBuild & AICTE

What it is

A retrieval-augmented assistant: your question is matched against a curated sustainability knowledge base using TF-IDF term weighting and cosine similarity, then the top matches are composed into an answer. Nothing is invented beyond what the knowledge base contains.

Two ways it can run

When the Python/FastAPI backend is reachable, EcoMind sends your question there and shows "Connected to backend" in the sidebar. If the backend isn't running — or you've opened this page on its own — EcoMind automatically switches to an equivalent TF-IDF engine built into the page itself, and shows "Offline mode." Either way, the retrieval logic and knowledge base are the same; only where the computation happens changes.

Transparency

Every assistant answer lists the topic tags of the knowledge-base entries it drew from, so you can see exactly what informed the response instead of trusting it blindly. The sidebar always shows which mode answered your last request.

Fairness

The knowledge base favors general, low-cost actions over ones that assume home ownership, high income, or a particular climate, and the footprint estimator uses global average factors rather than one country's assumptions.

Privacy

In offline mode, everything runs in your browser and nothing leaves the page. In backend mode, your question or footprint inputs are sent only to the EcoMind backend you've configured, purely to compute the answer — nothing is stored server-side.

Limitations

Footprint figures are simplified planning estimates from public average emission factors, not certified carbon accounting. The assistant can only answer from its ~48-entry knowledge base — it will say so when nothing matches well.
